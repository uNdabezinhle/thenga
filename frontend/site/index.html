Reka Ads

Reka sells and places operated ads. You can switch a placement off. You cannot edit Reka’s creative. A published share of that revenue is paid to the shop. The opening share is 30%, and it is shown before you opt in.

Self-serve banners are a paid extra. Reka does not pay you for an ad you bought from Reka. Ads are about the shop and the category, not a named shopper’s history.

Open a shop

This creates your account and an empty shop. It does not copy the Northbound catalogue, and it does not charge the prices on this page. A 14-day trial has both the shop and the till. When you are ready, the owner confirms a package and sales follow that package. Saved products stay if you change it.

Legal

The public name still needs a company search, a trademark search in classes 9, 35 and 42, and the domain, before a public launch. These notices describe how the software behaves. Have them reviewed before you rely on them with customers.

Terms

Reka is the platform. The merchant is the seller. The sample shop, Northbound, is a demonstration. Package prices on this site are illustrative and are not collected. Card and EFT stay on the sandbox until the merchant saves PayFast keys. Cash and card-present on the till do not need those keys. After the trial, the owner confirms a package. New sales follow that package. Downgrading keeps the catalogue and the orders already saved.

Privacy

An account stores the name, email, and password hash. An order stores what was bought, the address given at checkout, and the payment result. The mail log records sent, skipped, and failed messages for that shop. Marketing mail is sent only when the person has consented. Operated ads use the shop and the category, not a named shopper’s history.

POPIA

For a shopper’s order, the merchant is the responsible party and Reka processes the platform mail and the hosted record. Transactional mail about verify, payment, and the order is part of the service. Marketing mail needs consent under section 69. A person can ask the merchant for the record the shop holds.

Run a server

Copy the database volume and the media directory before an upgrade. Restore by putting those two back and starting the stack. Receipts are generated from orders, not stored as files. The mail log is on the merchant account. Leave PayFast keys empty until you are ready. Card and EFT then stay on the sandbox.

On an empty server, python manage.py setup_reka --trading-name "Kiosk" --owner-email owner@kiosk.test --password "a-long-secret" --package shop_till opens one shop and sends the verification mail. It does not load Northbound. Add --demo only for the sample catalogue.
