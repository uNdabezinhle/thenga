Reka Ads

Reka sells and places operated ads. You can switch a placement off. You cannot edit Reka’s creative. A published share of that revenue is paid to the shop. The opening share is 30%, and it is shown before you opt in.

Self-serve banners are a paid extra. Reka does not pay you for an ad you bought from Reka. Ads are about the shop and the category, not a named shopper’s history.

Open a shop

Create an account in the sample app, then send your trading name, package, and extras to POST /api/v1/platform/onboarding/. The new shop starts empty. It does not copy Northbound. The checklist is GET /api/v1/platform/onboarding/status/. Packages are listed at GET /api/v1/platform/packages/.

Legal

The public name still needs a company search, a trademark search in classes 9, 35 and 42, and the domain, before a public launch. Transactional mail about your order and payment is part of the service. Marketing mail is sent only with consent. Operated ads are not built from a personal profile.
